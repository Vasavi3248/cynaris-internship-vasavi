### Key Learnings
- **SELECT:** Retrieves specific columns or all columns from a table.
- **WHERE:** Filters records based on a condition.
- **ORDER BY:** Sorts query results in ascending or descending order.
- **BETWEEN:** Selects values within a specified range.
- **SUM():** Calculates the total of numeric values.
- **COUNT():** Counts records.
- **GROUP BY:** Groups records to calculate summaries for each category.
- **Calculated columns:** Perform calculations in a query, such as quantity multiplied by price to calculate revenue.

### Practical Application
